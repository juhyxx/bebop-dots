# Drum sample credits and licenses

The drums are the **vdrummer Jazz kit** (<https://github.com/juhyxx/vdrummer>): one-shot hits made by
vdrummer's `tools/build_samples.py` from the sample libraries below. For each instrument it picks a hard
hit (and for some a softly played one, `*-soft.wav`), mixes it down to mono, trims it to start on the
hit and end once it has died away (with a fade), and scales it to a set peak level. The sources are
credited below; their licenses apply to these files.

Files: `kick`, `kick-soft`, `hhc` (closed hi-hat), `hhc-soft`, `hhp` (pedal hi-hat), `ride` and `rim`
(side stick) come from AVL Blonde Bop; `snare` and `snare-soft` are the brushed snare from MuseScore_General.
